Astro

Three apps that run on your own computer and work together: your calendar, your conversations and a team of AI agents that keeps the work moving.

AstroCalendar: all your Google calendars in one week, meeting transcripts you can search, and busy time kept in step across accounts.
AstroChat: WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram and more in one inbox, with every person behind each conversation.
Astrobot: AI agents that read what matters, follow your rules, and ask before they act.

Astro is local-first: the apps are installed on your machine and keep your data there. There is no Astro cloud account and no Astro server that stores your calendars, mail or messages.
